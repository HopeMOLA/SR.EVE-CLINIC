Preparing for your appointment

General suggestions. The clinic may add specific instructions for some services.

  • Bring a list of any medication you currently take.
  • Bring any relevant previous health records, if you have them.
  • Note down your questions so you can cover them during your visit.
  • Arrive a few minutes early.

Frequently asked questions

How do I request an appointment?
Use any “Book an Appointment” button, complete the short request form and the clinic will contact you to confirm availability. A request is not a confirmed booking until the clinic confirms it.
What are your opening hours?
We are open Monday – Friday, 08:30 to 19:00, and Saturday – Sunday, 09:00 to 14:00.
Which services do you offer?
You can see everything the clinic offers on our services page.
Do you accept medical aid, and how do I pay?
Payment options and medical aid information will be added here once confirmed by the clinic. Please ask when you contact us.
How do I change or cancel an appointment?
Please contact the clinic as early as you can using the details in the contact section so the time can be offered to someone else.
How is my personal information protected?
We only ask for what is needed to respond to your request. Read the privacy notice for details.
What if I have a medical emergency?
This website is not for emergencies and is not monitored in real time. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency facility.
